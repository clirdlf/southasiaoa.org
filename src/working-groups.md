## [Content Curation Working Group](/content-creation-working-group/)

This group solicits ideas for new SAOA content from scholars and librarians, conducts research on available holdings, and selects and prioritizes materials for Executive Board consideration. Responsibilities include:

- Consulting with digital projects and archives for potential content
- Selecting titles for inclusion and assessing copyright status
- Working with other SAOA groups to support the digital resource mission

## [Funding Working Group](/funding-working-group/)

This group advises the Executive Board on financial matters, including:

- Fundraising from external sources
- Developing financial plans and cash flow projections
- Recruiting new institutional members

## [Infrastructure Working Group](/infrastructure-working-group/)

This group supports the technology backbone of SAOA. Focus areas include:

- Administration, staffing, and partnerships
- Site improvements and operational standards
- Digitization and metadata practices
- Preservation and long-term storage
- Project-based tasks like QA and ingest handling
- Adapting to emerging technology needs (e.g., images, datasets, archives)

## [Outreach Working Group](/outreach-working-group/)

This group promotes SAOA and helps shape its public presence. Activities include:

- User engagement and community building
- Developing sustainable outreach strategies
- Creating content and workflows for social media
- Diversifying the SAOA user base
